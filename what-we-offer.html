Path One

The Fast, Fair Cash Offer

We buy inherited and probate property for our own account, at a fair value grounded in an independent appraisal. Because our funds are ready, we can often close within days of the estate being able to convey clear title.

You pay no commissions and no fees to us. What you see on the closing statement is what the estate receives. And if an urgent cost arrives before closing, an advance can bridge it, credited to your proceeds at the closing table.

  • No showings, no open houses, no strangers walking through
